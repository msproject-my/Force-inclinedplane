OBJECT ON AN INCLINED PLANE

Resolution of Forces • Vector Axis • Normal Force (R) & Weight Components (Wx, Wy)
ENGINEERING SCIENCE
1

PRINCIPLE OF INCLINED FORCES (flat vs elevated surface)

CONCEPT
Core Physics Principle: All force vectors (R, W, Wx, Wy) originate at a single central point inside object (m). As angle θ increases, Normal force (R) rotates perpendicular to the plane while Weight (W = mg) remains strictly vertical down.
STEP 1: FLAT SURFACE θ = 0°
Normal Force (R):R = W = mg
Sliding Force (Wx):Wx = 0 N
STEP 2: INCLINED SURFACE θ > 0°
Normal Component:R = Wy = mg cosθ
Parallel Component:Wx = mg sinθ
2

FORCE RESOLUTION & FORMULAS

FORMULAS
PRIMARY VECTOR COMPONENT EQUATIONS
Parallel / Downslope Force
Wx = m · g · sin θ
Perpendicular / Normal Reaction Force
Wy = m · g · cos θ
💡
Memory Trick (Polytechnic Mnemonic):
🏃 Wx (Sliding Down) = Uses SIN θ
🧘 Wy (Perpendicular Axis) = Uses COS θ
CASE A: SMOOTH INCLINE f = 0 N

Object accelerates downslope purely due to component Wx without resistive friction force.

Fnet = Wx = m · a
m · g · sinθ = m · a ⇒ a = g · sinθ
CASE B: ROUGH INCLINE fk = μ·R

Friction force (fk = μR) opposes downslope sliding motion along the surface.

Fnet = Wx - fk = m · a
mg sinθ - μ(mg cosθ) = m · a
